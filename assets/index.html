1820, a winter masquerade. The youngest son of a fallen marquis was bitten beneath blue lanterns by a woman named Delphine. His body stopped forever somewhere between boy and man.

1924, Paris in the Années Folles. While jazz and electric light flood the night, Aurel is still treated as “a talented child.” He wants only one thing — for someone to see him as he truly is: a man.
